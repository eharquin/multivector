# Research Impact Evidence

**Status:** No impact claim has yet been accepted into this record.

This log records verifiable research use and community influence for releases,
project reporting, and a possible JOSS research-impact statement. Planned use,
GitHub stars, download counts, and feature breadth are not sufficient evidence
on their own.

## Accepted evidence categories

- published articles, preprints, theses, posters, talks, or course/research
  material that use MultiVector;
- reproducible analyses, constructions, figures, or benchmarks produced with a
  named release;
- documented adoption, evaluation, or integration by a group outside the
  original project;
- public research-use issues and feature changes traceable to user feedback;
- presentations or demonstrations to a relevant research community;
- comparative results that substantiate a claimed capability or workflow.
