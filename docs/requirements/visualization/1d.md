# 1D Visualization Requirements

**Status:** Draft for review
**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Applies to:** Any algebra definition modeling 1D geometry
**License:** MIT

## 1. Scope

This document defines rendering and interaction shared by 1D algebra models.
Algebra-specific interpretation shall produce renderer-independent entities
before this layer.

- [ ] **VIZ1D-001:** The visualizer shall provide a graduated axis and visible
  origin.
- [ ] **VIZ1D-002:** It shall render supported points, positioned vectors, labels,
  colors, visibility, and lists through one primitive pipeline.
- [ ] **VIZ1D-003:** Unsupported entities and render truncation shall be reported.
- [ ] **VIZ1D-004:** Algebraic results shall not depend on viewport state.
- [ ] **VIZ1D-005:** A positioned vector shall render as an oriented segment from
  `position` to derived `head`, with direction conveyed by geometry as well as
  color. A zero vector shall retain a selectable origin marker and label.
- [ ] **VIZ1D-006:** Points, shafts, heads, handles, labels, and axis marks shall be
  render primitives produced from semantic entities and appearance; they shall
  contain no algebra backend value or algebra identifier.
- [ ] **VIZ1D-007:** Lists shall preserve element order and identity, use the same
  primitives as individual entities, and expose an explicit deterministic
  render limit governed by the shared limits specification. Omitted elements
  shall be counted in a textual diagnostic.
- [ ] **VIZ1D-008:** Visibility hides geometry and its handles but does not suppress
  evaluation or diagnostics. Label visibility is independent of geometry
  visibility and shall follow the shared accessible-name policy in the
  [limits and interaction constants](../../specifications/limits-and-constants.md).

## 2. Viewport and interaction

- [ ] **VIEW1D-001:** Pointer conversion shall use an explicit viewport transform.
- [ ] **VIEW1D-002:** Pan and zoom shall preserve mathematical coordinates.
- [ ] **VIEW1D-003:** `Centering` shall center the origin without changing scale.
- [ ] **VIEW1D-004:** `Box` shall resize the layout without changing mathematical
  viewport coordinates or entering document state.

INTERACT1D-001 has **Core** commitment for accessible selection. INTERACT1D-002
through INTERACT1D-006 have **Milestone** commitment and apply only when a
milestone includes direct manipulation.

- [ ] **INTERACT1D-001:** Supported entities shall expose accessible selection and
  manipulation handles.
- [ ] **INTERACT1D-002:** A pointer gesture shall request a geometric edit; the
  domain shall decide whether and how source may be rewritten.
- [ ] **INTERACT1D-003:** Dragging a vector body requests translation of its
  position; dragging its head requests a value edit with position fixed.
  Dragging a point requests its single coordinate edit. Each request identifies
  the semantic entity, degree of freedom, target coordinate, and gesture phase.
- [ ] **INTERACT1D-004:** Preview updates during one drag shall coalesce into one
  document command on release. Escape or pointer cancellation restores the
  pre-gesture state. An unsupported inverse edit shall make no source change and
  shall expose the refusal reason.
- [ ] **INTERACT1D-005:** Keyboard manipulation shall offer the same degrees of
  freedom using the small and large coordinate increments in the shared limits
  and interaction constants. It shall use the same geometric-edit and
  source-rewrite path as pointer manipulation.
- [ ] **INTERACT1D-006:** Snapping, if enabled, shall be visible, reversible, and
  defined in mathematical rather than screen coordinates. The initial workflow
  shall not require snapping.

## 3. Accessibility

The common accessibility requirements A11Y-001 through A11Y-009 apply to the 1D
visualizer. In particular, the keyboard, non-color, accessible-name, target-size,
status-announcement, and reduced-motion requirements apply to its axis,
entities, handles, viewport controls, diagnostics, and animation. This section
does not redefine those requirements.
