# 2D Visualization Requirements

**Status:** Draft for review
**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Applies to:** Initial VGA 2D vertical slice
**License:** MIT

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the minimum shared guarantees needed to discover the
initial two-dimensional visual workflow through implementation. It is not a
complete design for every future 2D entity or interaction. Findings from the
vertical slice may revise these draft requirements before acceptance.

Algebra-specific interpretation produces renderer-independent semantic entities
before this layer. The visualizer consumes those entities and never backend
algebra values.

## 2. Foundation rendering

- **VIZ2D-001:** The initial visualizer shall provide a two-dimensional
  mathematical viewport with visible origin and axes.
- **VIZ2D-002:** It shall render supported points, positioned vectors,
  positioned oriented areas, labels, appearance, visibility, and lists through
  one primitive pipeline.
- **VIZ2D-003:** Algebraic results shall not depend on viewport state.
- **VIZ2D-004:** A positioned vector with a nonzero grade-one value shall render
  as an oriented segment from its position to its derived head. Direction shall
  not be conveyed by color alone. The all-zero multivector shall receive the
  same canonical scalar interpretation regardless of source construction and
  shall retain a textual state without a spatial vector marker.
- **VIZ2D-005:** Render primitives shall contain no algebra backend value or
  algebra identifier.
- **VIZ2D-006:** Unsupported entities and render truncation shall be reported in
  text without altering the evaluated mathematical result.
- **VIZ2D-007:** Construction provenance and object appearance may select
  presentation defaults, but shall not change the standard geometric
  interpretation of an owned multivector value.
- **VIZ2D-008:** A nonzero pure VGA(2) bivector shall render at its separately
  evaluated position as an oriented loop whose mathematical enclosed area is
  the absolute bivector value. Positive and negative values shall communicate
  counter-clockwise and clockwise orientation without relying on color. When
  safely available, direct `V ^ W` construction provenance may instead select
  the corresponding oriented parallelogram, preserving the same area
  convention. Text and accessible SVG descriptions shall identify the signed
  value, orientation, area, and position.

## 3. Viewport

- **VIEW2D-001:** Screen and mathematical coordinates shall be connected by one
  explicit, testable viewport transform.
- **VIEW2D-002:** Pan, zoom, and layout resize shall not change mathematical
  values or coordinates.
- **VIEW2D-003:** Resetting the view shall center the mathematical origin using a
  documented default scale.
- **VIEW2D-004:** Continuous zoom shall remain centered on the pointer's
  mathematical coordinate and shall be bounded from 8 through 512 reference
  pixels per mathematical unit. Reset uses 72 reference pixels per unit.
- **VIEW2D-005:** Major grid spacing shall follow a deterministic 1–2–5 × 10^n
  progression with bounded subdivisions and generated line counts. Numeric
  labels shall use stable precision without floating-point artifacts.
- **VIEW2D-006:** Grid, axes and axis labels, graduations, and object scale shall
  be independently controllable through persistent view state. View commands
  shall not create or replace mathematical undo-history entries.
- **VIEW2D-007:** Pointer and keyboard input shall provide equivalent pan, zoom,
  and reset outcomes. Resizing shall retain the visible mathematical center.

## 4. Interaction

The interaction requirements below have **Milestone** commitment and apply only
to the VGA 2D Visual Workflow milestone.

- **INTERACT2D-001:** Double-clicking empty viewport space shall create a named
  VGA(2) vector declaration using the mathematical coordinate under the
  pointer. Rendered entities are not directly selectable by clicking them.
- **INTERACT2D-002:** Viewport creation shall use the shared inverse viewport
  transform and deterministic source precision. Pan, zoom, reset, and layout
  changes shall not alter the mathematical coordinate represented by a click.
- **INTERACT2D-003:** Generated declaration names shall not collide with an
  existing declaration, built-in, or reserved VGA name.
- **INTERACT2D-004:** One creation gesture shall produce one semantic insertion
  command and at most one document-history entry. Focus moves to the created
  expression; undo returns focus to the viewport and redo restores expression
  focus.
- **INTERACT2D-005:** Double-clicking a rendered object shall not create an
  expression. Creation success and refusal shall be announced in text to
  assistive technology.
- **INTERACT2D-006:** Eligible vector and bivector bases shall expose a shared
  solid point with a translucent movable contour. Moving a base shall edit only
  its position source; a locked base shall retain only the solid point.
- **INTERACT2D-007:** An eligible direct vector head shall retain its arrowhead
  and expose a thin unfilled interaction ring only on hover or keyboard focus.
  Moving the head shall edit its vector components with the base fixed.
- **INTERACT2D-008:** Pointer manipulation shall use capture and one preview
  transaction. Completion creates one undo entry; Escape, pointer cancellation,
  or lost capture restores the exact pre-gesture document.
- **INTERACT2D-009:** Every movable base or head shall provide keyboard arrow
  movement with small and Shift-modified large increments and textual status
  announcements.
- **INTERACT2D-010:** Rendered object bodies and list-rendered copies shall not
  become direct manipulation or selection targets. Coincident and overlapping
  interaction targets shall resolve deterministically.
- **INTERACT2D-011:** Dragging an eligible object base within a fixed
  screen-space radius of a named vector or bivector base, or a named vector
  head, shall preview and create a dynamic `.position` or `.head`
  position-source reference. Viewport zoom shall not change the acquisition
  radius.
- **INTERACT2D-012:** Anchor acquisition shall exclude the dragged object,
  anonymous and list-rendered objects, and any relationship that would produce
  an invalid or cyclic position dependency. Refusal shall preserve the exact
  pre-gesture document and be announced textually.
- **INTERACT2D-013:** Anchor preview shall use shape as well as color, retain the
  current candidate through a larger release radius, and resolve ties by stable
  target identity and anchor kind.
- **INTERACT2D-014:** Dragging a linked base away shall replace the relationship
  with its resolved literal position. Linking and unlinking shall each form one
  atomic undo/redo entry.
- **INTERACT2D-015:** A focused movable base shall support cycling valid anchors
  with Enter and unlinking with Delete or Backspace, with textual announcements
  equivalent to the pointer workflow.

## 5. Deferred design

The initial slice does not commit to direct object selection, rotors, arbitrary
higher-grade entities, cameras, per-element list appearance, or
user-authored interpretations. These receive requirements only when prototypes
or a delivery milestone need them.

A one-dimensional visualizer is also deferred and is not a prerequisite for
this document or the initial public workflow.
